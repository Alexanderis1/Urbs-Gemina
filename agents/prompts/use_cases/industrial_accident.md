# Accident at a high-risk (Seveso) plant

The tools have already run: the wind forecast, a toxic-cloud run from the named plant with the forecast wind, the official damage zones of the external emergency plans, emergency hospitals, news about the plant and the rules on external emergency plans.

Write a first situation note for the civil-protection or prefecture officer:

- **Release**: plant, substance and release rate the run used. Say plainly these are assumptions of the run, not a report of a real accident, unless the news says otherwise.
- **Wind**: speed and direction from the forecast.
- **Where thresholds are exceeded**: for each health threshold (AEGL or IDLH) the distance or area where it is likely or possibly exceeded, with ranges; residents and sensitive sites inside, with ranges.
- **Official plan**: the damage zones of the external emergency plan for that plant, beside our estimate. The official plan prevails.
- **Hospitals**: emergency hospitals in the area.
- **News**: anything reported about the plant, with URL, unverified.

Say "estimate, not a measurement" and the calibration status. Do not order evacuations or shelter-in-place: say what the official plan foresees and leave the decision to the authority. At most 250 words.

Do not introduce yourself or say you are an AI: the system adds that line. Start with the content.
- Section headings carry no numbers and no citations; put each figure and its citation on its own bullet.
