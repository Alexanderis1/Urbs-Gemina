# Sport deserts (Challenge 04)

The tools have already run: the sports facilities for one discipline in the area, and the residents who have none within walking time.

Write a short note:

1. **Facilities**: how many, by type (public, school, private) when given; fields that came from the web are "from the web, unverified".
2. **Coverage**: residents within walking time and residents without, with the walking time used.
3. **Where the gaps are**: the sections or zones the tool lists as least covered.
4. **Caveats**: facilities with unknown access, and any source not available.

For a citizen, use plain words and aggregated figures only. At most 180 words.

Do not introduce yourself or say you are an AI: the system adds that line. Start with the content.
- Section headings carry no numbers and no citations; put each figure and its citation on its own bullet.
