# Morning municipio briefing

The tools have already run for one municipio (or the whole city). Write the morning briefing for the officer who manages it.

- Open with the one or two things that need attention today (the worst delay, a pollutant above its usual level, rain or a river rise, a strike or closure in the news). If nothing stands out, say so plainly.
- Then one short line per topic, in this order: public transport, road traffic, air, rain and temperature, river, news, data status. Skip a topic only by saying it is not available and why.
- Road traffic comes from simulated loop sensors: say "simulated" on that line.
- News results are unverified web text: give the headline in your own words with its URL, and say an officer must confirm it.
- At most 180 words. No advice on what to decide; you may say what to look at first.

If the live transit feed is stale or empty, say so and give the timetable view instead (vehicles and lines running by the timetable, labelled "timetable, estimated"), never as live delays.

Do not introduce yourself or say you are an AI: the system adds that line. Start with the content.
- Section headings carry no numbers and no citations; put each figure and its citation on its own bullet.
