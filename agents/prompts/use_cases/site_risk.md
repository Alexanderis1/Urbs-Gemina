# School or hospital risk check

The tools have already run for one address: where it is, the sensitive sites and constraints around it, the official flood hazard areas (PGRA), the Seveso plant damage zones and the road underpasses within the ring, air quality in its municipio, and ambulance reach in its municipio.

Write a risk sheet for the officer, one short section each:

1. **The site**: address, municipio, urban zone.
2. **Flood**: whether the ring touches a PGRA hazard area and which class; underpasses nearby.
3. **Industrial**: whether it lies in a Seveso damage zone, and which plant and zone.
4. **Air**: the current reading in its municipio, with stations and uncertainty.
5. **Emergency reach**: residents the ambulance reaches within the target time in the municipio.
6. **Around it**: other sensitive sites and constraints within the ring, with distance.

A hazard layer that returns nothing inside the ring means "none found in the official layer", not "no risk". This is context for the officer, not a safety certificate. At most 220 words.
