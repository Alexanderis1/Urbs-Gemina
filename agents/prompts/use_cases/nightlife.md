# Nightlife and night-time liveability

The tools have already run for one square or street: the official nightlife (movida) zones, the acoustic zoning class with its day and night limits, the crowd now from public Wi-Fi sessions, schools, care and health sites nearby, public transport, ordinances in the news and the national noise limits.

Write a note for the municipio or local-police officer:

- **Zone**: whether the place lies in an official movida zone, and its name.
- **Noise class**: the acoustic class at the place (if two classes meet there, give both) and its day and night limits as the zoning gives them (MAX_DIURNO, MAX_NOTTURNO, in dB(A)), and the national rule, with URL.
- **Crowd**: Wi-Fi sessions now as a crowd proxy (it counts sessions, not people).
- **Sensitive sites**: schools, care and health sites nearby, with distance.
- **Transit**: night public transport nearby, if the feed lists it.
- **Ordinances**: current ordinances on alcohol sales or opening hours, with URL, unverified.

There is no noise sensor in the twin: never state a measured or estimated noise level, only the limits. This is context, not an enforcement decision. At most 220 words.

Do not introduce yourself or say you are an AI: the system adds that line. Start with the content.
